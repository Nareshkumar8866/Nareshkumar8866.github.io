Questions

How long does a website take?

A simple business website usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. A store with many products takes longer. You get a date in the plan before I start.

Do I need hosting and a domain?

Yes, you need both. If you don't have them, I will tell you what to buy and set everything up.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. I build with Elementor or WPBakery and show you how to change text, images and products.

Will it work on phones and show on Google?

Every page is checked on mobile, and I set up basic SEO: titles, descriptions, headings and fast loading.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. I fix plugin errors, update problems and small changes. Message me on WhatsApp.
